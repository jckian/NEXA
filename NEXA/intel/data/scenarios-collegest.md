## The forecast question

The parcel is a **surface parking lot directly beside the Metro A Line Chinatown station**
(station opened 2003; it sits on Spring St above College St). In 2023 the Riboli family
(San Antonio Winery) proposed a 5-storey, ~225,000 sf Grimshaw-designed creative-office
building there. It is in environmental review and **needs a General Plan Amendment plus a
zone change** — which is itself the clearest available evidence that current zoning does
not permit the program.

So the question is not "what could go here" but **which of four resolutions of one tension
actually lands**: a speculative office bet, on the best-connected parcel in Chinatown, into
a ~22% office-vacancy market, in a district organizing hard against displacement.

## What "the site needs now" looks like here

Unlike City Market (where housing is the defensible answer), this parcel's honest
near-term answer is **housing or nothing**: a station-adjacent surface lot is the
lowest-value, highest-heat use of the best-connected land in Chinatown, and the office
program that would replace it is the one program the market is currently rejecting.
Scenario B is the transition database's answer; Scenario C is the community's. The
difference between them is who pays, not what fits.
